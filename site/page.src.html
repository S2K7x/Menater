An alert comes in. Five steps later, you decide.

Every alert goes through the same five steps, in the same order. The fourth one is you.

  1. Step 1 An alert arrives Something on your network looked wrong. Your security tools send it here.
  2. Step 2 Context is added Is this address known for attacks? Has this password leaked? MENATER asks around.
  3. Step 3 The AI gives its opinion What it thinks this is, how sure it is, and why. An opinion — not an order.
  4. Step 4 You You decide It asks you, on screen or in your chat app. Nothing happens until you answer.
  5. Step 5 It gets written down A record of what happened that nobody can quietly change afterwards.
  • You say yes That one thing happens. Nothing else.
  • You say no Nothing happens.
  • You do not answer Nothing happens. It goes to a person instead of expiring in silence.

On a fresh install, step 4 never even happens. MENATER only watches: it notes what it would have suggested, and does nothing at all, until you decide to switch that on.

Three things, on one screen.

Sorts your alerts

The urgent ones first, the rest out of your way. Open one and you see the whole story on a single page.

Checks your code

Point it at a folder or a GitHub project and it looks for the mistakes an attacker would use. Each one says what could happen, and how to fix it.

Answers a question

Paste an address, a website or an email and ask what is known about it. It always tells you how many sources actually replied.

What it will never do.

  • It never acts on its own. There are exactly four things it can do, and every one of them needs your yes first.
  • It never guesses. When it does not know something, it says so. It never fills a blank with a plausible answer.
  • It never hides a problem. Something that broke is never shown as fine. A screen exists whose only job is to find that.
  • It never phones home. No account, no tracking. Your alerts go to the AI you picked, and nowhere else.
