MENATER

מנטר

MENATER watches in the present, and says what it cannot see. From the Hebrew מנטר — menater, the present tense of lenater, to monitor. The product is not named after a technology. It is named after an action in progress.

The rules below are the ones already in the code
§1
The name

A verb, not a noun

Because the name is a verb in the present tense, three things follow, and they are not decoration: the brand speaks in the active voice; the console shows a state, never a finished report; and it is an act of watching, not of deciding — which is exactly the security model. It watches, a human acts.

§2
The idea

Legible restraint

Every competitor sells automated confidence. MENATER sells the opposite, and the product already argues it in five separate places in the code:

The product ruleWhere it lives
Never fill a gap with a default. Missing data is shown as missingThe two-tier alert contract
Silence is never consent — an approval timeout executes nothingThe approval flow
No irreversible action without an explicit human actThe closed action catalogue
A failure must never show greenThe Tracking tab exists only for this
The assistant cannot act, only readSeven getters, no writes

So the design has to carry that, or the design is lying about the product. Four consequences: show absence as a designed state; never reassure by default; look measured, not marketed; reward a second look.

§5
Colour

Five rules, in priority order

1 · One accent per screen

The acid green designates exactly one thing at a time. Two accents means neither is one.

2 · Red and orange are not decorative

Colour is processed pre-attentively — before reading. It is the fastest channel the interface has, and the easiest to spend on nothing. A red button teaches an analyst to ignore red.

3 · The palette is a variable, the roles are fixed

No rule outside themes.css knows a theme's name. A hardcoded colour will follow none of the six.

4 · Only “waiting for a human” carries the accent

The one state where the system is blocked and someone must act. Everything else uses the risk palette.

5 · Colour never carries meaning alone

WCAG 1.4.1. Every risk colour is paired with a word, and usually a shape — a pill, an inset bar, an icon.

The brand palette — grayed

Contrast is tooled, never judged by eye

ui-monospace · every value produced by the system

The monospace rule is semantic, not decorative. Mono means: this string came from the machine, and it is exact. Prose about a value is sans; the value is mono. That is why a fourth typeface would cost something — NN/g classes font variation that carries no meaning as extraneous cognitive load, a tax levied on an analyst scanning forty rows.

Fonts load from Google Fonts and fall back to system faces when the network is absent — a console on an isolated network must not lose its hierarchy because a CDN is unreachable. That is why the display fallback stack is condensed and heavy, not a default sans.

§7
Space

2 px base, one radius

Then 24 · 32 · 40 · 48 · 64 · 80 for layout. Nothing else. The scale is 2 px-based rather than the more fashionable 4 px because it was derived from the code, not imposed on it — 6 and 10 were already the two most common values in the sheet, and forcing fifty two-pixel shifts across screens nobody would re-review is a cost with no reader on the other end. What the scale removes is the genuinely rogue values: 1 · 3 · 5 · 9 · 14 · 18 · 22.

Radius is --radius: 4px, plus 50% for a dot; nothing between. Large radii are the loudest “consumer app” signal there is, and this product is not one. One exception: the switch track keeps a full capsule, because that shape is the signifier for a switch — a square one reads as a checkbox, which does something else.

Borders before shadows: a 1 px hairline separates, and the shadow is reserved for what actually floats — the assistant panel, a dialog, the sticky save bar. Panels do not float.

Density is a feature. The triage table is meant to show forty rows. Do not give it room to breathe — an analyst scrolling is an analyst who has lost the overview.

§10
Mark

The register

Five ticks, and the middle one is a stub rather than a full bar — the product's first principle drawn literally: a missing value keeps its place instead of being omitted. Absence sits at the centre, so the mark stays symmetric: the gap is the composition, not a defect in it.

The choiceThe reason
Angular, never roundedCircular shapes activate softness; angular ones activate hardness and durability, and read as more premium
SymmetricAsymmetry raises perceived excitement, and pays only for brands whose personality is excitement. This one's is competence
Describes the method, never the threatDescriptive logos raise brand evaluations — but the effect reverses for categories carrying negative associations, and security is one
No colour of its ownfill="currentColor", and the CSS sets color: var(--accent). One file follows all six themes

The geometry exists once, in MARK_BARS (src/components/Mark.tsx). The component renders it and theme/favicon.ts builds the tab icon from the same array, so the mark and its own favicon cannot become two different drawings. The tab icon follows the theme too — it is the one surface CSS cannot reach. Placement is settled: top left, clickable; 89% better recall than on the right.

§4
Voice

Name the thing, then the consequence

Write this
  • It refuses to run: no host to isolate
  • Nothing new for 5 minutes, outside an approval wait
  • 3 of 5 sources answered
  • This log appears to contain an injection attempt
  • Shadow mode: decisions are recorded, none are executed
  • The database is unreachable (ECONNREFUSED)
Not this
  • Action failed
  • Stalled ⚠️
  • Clean
  • Suspicious content detected!
  • Safe mode enabled
  • Oops — something went wrong

No exclamation marks, no emoji in product surfaces, no “Oops”. A triage console interrupts people at 3 a.m.; cheerfulness reads as not understanding the situation. And never say a thing is fine when nobody looked — Not asked is a different sentence from nothing found, and that difference is the whole product.

§12
Never

The anti-generic list

NN/g's 2026 finding is that polish has stopped being a quality signal: audiences now ask whether a person cared enough to design it themselves. These are the tells that answer no.

And the positive form, which matters more

Leave the decision visible. The comments in themes.css that explain why Attck's red was lowered, why --hero is an inset rather than an inversion, why Punk's accent was darkened — that is the evidence a person was here. It is currently readable only by someone who opens the CSS. Surface it: a shown decision is worth more than a claim of quality.
